Who we are

Helpendz is a student project by Computer Science Group 73 at the Informatics Institute of Technology (IIT), Sri Lanka, for the Agile Team Project module run with the University of Westminster. Contact: hello@helpendz.com.

What we collect and why

When you join the waitlist we collect:

  • your name;
  • a phone number or an email address;
  • whether you want to join as a customer or a service provider;
  • your area (Kalmunai, Nintavur or somewhere else);
  • the language you were using on the website (English, Sinhala or Tamil), so we write to you in that language.

We use these details only to tell you when Helpendz launches and, if you agree, to invite you to give feedback on the app. We do not sell or share your details with anyone else.

Legal basis

We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us. We follow the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 of Sri Lanka. Because the project is part of a UK university course, we also use the principles of the UK GDPR as a guide to good practice.

Where your details are stored and for how long

Details are stored in a Supabase database protected by access rules, so the public website can add a sign-up but cannot read any. Only the project team can view the list. We delete the list when the project ends or within 12 months, whichever is sooner.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your details at any time by emailing hello@helpendz.com. We will reply within 14 days.

Cookies, storage and analytics

This site sets no cookies and uses no advertising or tracking scripts. It remembers your language and light or dark mode in your own browser's local storage; nothing about that is sent to us. Fonts and images are served from this website, not from third parties.
