Why Helpendz

Finding help shouldn't take five phone calls.

Today

  • You ask neighbours or scroll WhatsApp groups for a number.
  • You call, and call again, to find someone who is free.
  • You can't check who is coming to your house.
  • You wait without knowing when they'll arrive.

With Helpendz

  • Providers near you, sorted by distance, with ratings and starting prices.
  • One request with a time, a note and a photo of the problem.
  • Every provider's ID is checked before they get the badge.
  • You see them on the map and confirm them with a code at the door.

How it works

From request to review

  1. Pick a category and see ID-verified providers near you, with ratings and starting prices.

  2. Choose a date and time, describe the problem and add a photo so they come prepared.

  3. Pay by card through PayHere once the provider accepts, or pay cash when the job is done.

  4. When they set off you see them on the map with an arrival time. Sharing stops when they arrive.

  5. Your app shows a six-digit arrival code. The provider enters it at your door to start the job.

  6. Rate the job so your neighbours can choose well, or report a problem.

Safety

The person at your door is the person you booked.

Helpendz is built around a few simple checks.

  • ID checked by a person

    Providers upload their National Identity Card and a selfie. An authorised Helpendz reviewer checks them before the “ID verified” badge appears.

  • ID details kept to a minimum

    We keep only a secure code and the last four digits of the NIC. ID photos are deleted 30 days after the review, and every view is logged.

  • A code only you hold

    The six-digit arrival code works only for your booking and locks after five wrong tries.

  • Location only during the trip

    You see the provider only while they're on the way. Sharing stops on arrival, if the booking is cancelled, or after three hours. No route is stored.

  • Card details never reach us

    Card payments go through PayHere. Helpendz never sees or stores your card number.

The badge confirms who someone is, not how good their work is. Ratings and reviews tell you that.

Emergency Mode

Burst pipe or flat tyre? Ask the nearest few at once.

One tap sends your request to the 3–5 nearest available, ID-verified providers. The first to accept takes the job, and you see them on the way.

For accidents, fire or medical emergencies, call the emergency services first: Police 119 · Ambulance (Suwa Seriya) 1990 · Fire and rescue 110. Helpendz connects you with service providers only.

Questions

Good questions, plain answers

When does Helpendz launch?

Coming soon. Join the waitlist and we'll message you when the app is ready in Kalmunai and Nintavur.

Does it cost anything for customers?

No. The app is free for customers. You pay the provider for the job, by card or cash.

Which areas do you cover?

Kalmunai and Nintavur first. We'll add nearby areas once these two work well.

How do you check providers?

Each provider submits their NIC and a selfie. An authorised reviewer checks them before the “ID verified” badge appears. The badge confirms who they are; ratings tell you about their work.

Is my location stored?

No location history is stored. A provider's live location is shown to you only while they're on the way to your booking.

What if the provider doesn't come?

You can cancel with a reason until the provider arrives, then book someone else. Requests that aren't accepted in time expire on their own.

How do I pay?

By card through PayHere, or in cash when the job is done. Card payments come with a receipt in the app.

I'm a provider. How do I join?

Join the waitlist as a service provider. When sign-ups open you'll verify your phone, upload your NIC and a selfie, and add your services and prices.
